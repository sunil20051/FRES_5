## Simulation study on wind energy generator.

## Aim:

To design and simulate a wind energy generator using PMSG in MATLAB/Simulink and obtain the output parameters

## Software Tool Used:

MATLAB 2021 or above

## Theory:

Wind power or wind energy is mostly the use of wind turbines to generate electricity. Wind power is a popular, sustainable, renewable energy source that has a much smaller impact on the environment than burning fossil fuels. Historically, wind power has been used in sails, windmills and windpumps but today it is mostly used to generate electricity. Wind farms consist of many individual wind turbines, which are connected to the electric power transmission network. Wind power is variable renewable energy, so power-management techniques are used to match supply and demand, such as: wind hybrid power systems, hydroelectric power or other dispatchable power sources, excess capacity, geographically distributed turbines, exporting and importing power to neighbouring areas, or grid storage. As the proportion of wind power in a region increases the grid may need to be upgraded. Weather forecasting allows the electric-power network to be readied for the predictable variations in production that occur.

## Procedure:

1.Open MATLAB<br>
2.From Simulink library browser, pick the following components<br>
a.Wind turbine, PMSG, three-phase RLC load<br>
b.Step, manual switch, constant, product<br>
c.Bus selector, scope, demux<br>
3.Connect the Simulink tools as shown in the circuit.<br>
4.Se the wind speed of the turbine as 1.2 p.u, pitch angle as 00, wind velocity as 12m/s, variable step wind velocity to 12m/s, 8m/s and 12m/s pitch angle value and the wind speed value as an input to the wind turbine.<br>
5.Run the simulator and obtain the graphs.<br>
